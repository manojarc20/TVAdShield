TVAdShield bundles the HEV native tunnel core in its Android APK. The native core is built in CI from the pinned upstream commits below.

Pinned source:
- hev-socks5-tunnel: d9dca26c7ad0e494492244f0309e80ee583e739e (MIT)
- hev-task-system: 328f35d903221b51811b3d02b277d665dfbdc75f (MIT)
- yaml: efa36117a8646d26d12b58e05bac472d7854a70d (MIT)
- lwip: e22c9d2873cd5a8aade9deb2e518b75dab05170e (BSD-3-Clause)
- hev-socks5-core: 162dd996299fc2d2bff2dd63728f8a2cd71ed31a (MIT)
